# Baseline Assessment: Team AI Readiness

## Purpose
This baseline assessment helps your team establish a starting point before beginning the DORA AI Paradox Book Club.
**Estimated Time: 10 Minutes**

## Individual Self-Assessment

### Current AI Usage
**1. How frequently do you interact with AI tools for development tasks?**
- [ ] Multiple times per day
- [ ] Daily
- [ ] A few times per week
- [ ] Rarely / Never

**2. The "Vacuum" Check: When AI saves you time on a task, where does that extra time usually go?**

- [ ] Learning/Skill development
- [ ] Meetings/Administrative tasks
- [ ] Helping others
- [ ] It doesn't feel like I have extra time

**3. How much do you trust the quality of AI-generated code?**

- [ ] High trust
- [ ] Moderate trust

1. **The Paradox Check:** Do we feel more productive individually, but still feel like shipping software is hard?
2. **What metric should we track to measure AI's impact?** (e.g., Change Failure Rate, PR Size, Deploy Freq)
