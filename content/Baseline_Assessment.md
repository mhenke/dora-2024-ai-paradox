## Next Steps

After completing this assessment:

1. **Identify your team's starting point** - What are your current strengths and weaknesses?
2. **Select one core metric to track** - This will be your measure of AI's impact throughout the book club
3. **Establish your baseline** - Document current values for your chosen metric
4. **Set expectations** - Discuss what success looks like for your team
