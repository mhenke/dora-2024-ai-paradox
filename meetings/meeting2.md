## Agenda

### Phase I: Preamble &amp; Systems View (5 min)

**Recap Meeting 1**:
- We saw the paradox: Individual gains but organizational instability
- **Why?** Systems haven't evolved to safely manage AI-accelerated speed

**Today's Focus**:
> "AI success is a **systems problem, not a tools problem**.
> 
> The DORA AI Capabilities Model identifies **7 foundational practices** that amplify AI's positive impact on organizational performance.
> 
> Today we focus on the **highest-leverage** capabilities: Platform, VSM, Data, and Governance."

**Set expectation**: This is about WHERE to invest, not just WHAT to do.

### Phase II: Foundational Prerequisite: Platforms (25 min)

**Paired Discussion: The Essential Foundation**

**Present the finding** (5 min):
- **90% of organizations** have adopted platform engineering.
- **Quality Internal Platforms** are the **essential foundation** for AI success.
- **Figure 44**: AI adoption has a **negligible effect** on organizational performance when platform quality is low.

**Discussion** (20 min):
1.  **Two Purposes**: Discuss how a platform serves as:
    - Distribution/Governance layer.
    - Risk mitigator/Safe space for experimentation.
2.  **Scenario**: "If we know our platform is poor (rated 1-2), what are the immediate risks of proceeding with AI?"
3.  **Leadership Imperative**: Treat the platform as an internal product.

### Phase III: Strategic Force Multiplier: VSM (15 min)

**Full Team Analysis**

**Present the finding** (5 min):
- **VSM acts as a force multiplier** for AI investments.
- VSM ensures localized AI productivity gains are applied to the **system's constraint**.
- **Figure 51**: VSM moderates the relationship between AI adoption and organizational performance.

**Discussion** (10 min):
- **The VSM Hypothesis**: "If AI is applied to a non-bottleneck, is that productivity wasted, or does it lead to chaos?"
- **Diagnostic**: "Do we know our system's constraint? If not, we are likely optimizing locally."

### Phase IV: Governance and Data Enablers (15 min)

**Structured Activity: Capability Self-Assessment**

**Present the Enablers** (5 min):
- **Clear AI Stance**: Amplifies effectiveness/performance, decreases friction.
- **Healthy Data Ecosystems**: Amplifies organizational performance.
- **AI-Accessible Internal Data**: Amplifies individual effectiveness.

**Vote &amp; Discuss** (10 min):
1.  **Vote**: "Which of these 3 is our weakest link?"
2.  **Discuss**:
    - **Clear Stance**: "Does lack of clarity cause us to act too conservatively or too permissively?"
    - **Data**: "How would accessing internal data change our daily work?"

### Phase V: Wrap &amp; Transition (5 min)

**Key Takeaways**:
1. **Platform quality** is the essential foundation - low quality = negligible AI benefit
2. **VSM** ensures local speed translates to organizational value
3. **Data** unification and **governance** clarity amplify AI's impact

## Facilitator Notes

**If participants struggle with abstract Capabilities**:
- **Anchor on Quantitative Impact ($1 Million Test)**:
  > "Let's look at the ROI. A clear AI stance **decreases friction** and amplifies **organizational performance**.
  > **Question**: If we had $1 million to spend, would we spend it on defining this foundational policy (high leverage) or just buying more AI licenses (low leverage without policy)?"

**If discussion stalls on Platform Quality**:
- Prompt: "Think about the last time you needed to deploy something. Was it self-service or did you wait for another team?"
- Ask: "On a scale of 1-5, how much friction do you experience with our internal tools?"
- Guide to Figure 44: "The data shows low platform quality means AI benefits don't scale"

**If VSM discussion gets too abstract**:
- **Don't**: Try to teach VSM mapping methodology
- **Do**: Keep focus on strategic outcome - "Do we know where our bottlenecks are?"
- Example: "If AI helps us code 2x faster but we still wait 2 days for code review, where should we optimize?"

**If no one did the reading**:
- Spend 10 min on Figure 44 (platform) live - this is the most critical
- Show Figure 51 quickly (VSM concept)
- Skip detailed capability definitions
- Focus all time on the scenario: "Is our platform good enough for AI?"

**If you run over time**:
- Priority: Platform discussion (15 min) > VSM (10 min) > Data &amp; Governance (combined 10 min) > Preamble
- Platform is NON-NEGOTIABLE—must address "what if quality is poor?"
- Can combine Data Ecosystems + AI-Accessible Data into single 10-min discussion

**Addressing user's question on platform risks** (10-min dedicated time):
Ensure this specific scenario analysis happens:
1. Show Figure 44's inverse relationship curve
2. Ask directly: "If we rated our platform 1-2, what happens when we push AI harder?"
   - (Expected: Faster generation → more deployment failures → instability increase)
3. Forced choice: "Should we invest in platform FIRST or continue AI rollout?"
4. Capture honest assessment: "Where is our platform quality TODAY?"

**Keeping VSM discussion focused** (addressing user's question):
Use this framing to avoid methodology rabbit holes:
> "VSM is about having a systems-level view. You don't need to create a map today.
> The question is: Do you know where your constraints are?
> Because AI will make us faster everywhere EXCEPT the constraints. That's wasted speed."

**Time allocation for Platform vs. VSM**:
- Platform: 15 min (longer because of scenario analysis)
- VSM: 10 min (ruthlessly focused on strategic outcome)
- Data + Governance: 15 min total (combined)
This ensures the "force multiplier" capabilities get adequate depth.
