# Meeting 3: Strategy & Success

## Overview
The final meeting synthesizes actionable strategies. We will focus on the new **DORA AI Capabilities Model** and the critical role of **Platform Engineering** in unlocking AI's value.

## Reading Required
*   **Source 1**: 2024 DORA Report, Pages 69-76 (Applying Insights)
*   **Source 3**: 2025 DORA Report, Pages 49-64 (DORA AI Capabilities Model & Platform Engineering)

## Facilitator Preparation
**Date**: [Date] | **Duration**: 60 min

*   [ ] Send reading reminder 1 week before.
*   [ ] Review the 7 Capabilities in the DORA AI Capabilities Model.
*   [ ] Prepare to discuss "Platform as a Product".

## Agenda

### 1. The DORA AI Capabilities Model (20 min)
*   **Review**: The 7 foundational capabilities that amplify AI's impact:
    1.  Clear AI Policy
    2.  Healthy Data Ecosystem
    3.  User-Centric Focus
    4.  Quality Internal Platform
    5.  ...and others.
*   **Discussion**: Which of these 7 capabilities is our strongest? Which is our weakest?

### 2. Platform Engineering as a Foundation (15 min)
*   **Review**:
    *   90% of organizations have adopted platform engineering.
    *   A high-quality platform is essential for AI success.
*   **Discussion**: Do we have a "paved road" for development? Does our platform reduce cognitive load?

### 3. Fostering Trust (10 min)
*   **Review**: Strategies to address low trust (policies, feedback, exposure).
*   **Discussion**: Has our trust in AI changed over the last 6 weeks?

### 4. Measuring Success (10 min)
*   **Review**: Moving beyond simple metrics (like "lines of code") to outcomes (Flow, Satisfaction, Value).
*   **Discussion**: What metrics should we track going forward?

### 5. Wrap-up & Commitment (5 min)
*   Final thoughts on the book club series.
*   Commitment to one personal or team change based on what we've learned.

## Key Questions
*   Do we have the foundational capabilities (like a good platform) to support AI?
*   Are we treating our internal platform as a product?
