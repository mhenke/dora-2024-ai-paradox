# Meeting 3: Strategy & Success

## Overview
The final meeting synthesizes actionable strategies. We will focus on the new **DORA AI Capabilities Model** and the critical role of **Platform Engineering** in unlocking AI's value.

*   **Source 1** - 2024 DORA Report: Pages 69-76 (Applying Insights)
    - File: `2024-dora-accelerate-state-of-devops-report.pdf`
*   **Source 2** - 2025 DORA Report: Pages 49-64 (DORA AI Capabilities Model & Platform Engineering)
    - File: `2025_state_of_ai_assisted_software_development.pdf`

## Agenda

### 1. The DORA AI Capabilities Model (20 min)
*   **Review**: The 7 foundational capabilities that amplify AI's impact:
    1.  **Clear and Communicated AI Stance** - Organization's official position on AI use
    2.  **Healthy Data Ecosystems** - Quality, accessible, unified internal data
    3.  **AI-Accessible Internal Data** - AI tools connected to internal systems
    4.  **Strong Version Control Practices** - Frequent commits and rollback capability
    5.  **Working in Small Batches** - Breaking changes into manageable units
    6.  **User-Centric Focus** - Keeping end users at center of development
    7.  **Quality Internal Platforms** - High-quality developer experience infrastructure
*   **Discussion**: Which of these 7 capabilities is our strongest? Which is our weakest?

### 2. Platform Engineering as a Foundation (15 min)
*   **Review**:
    *   90% of organizations have adopted platform engineering.
    *   A high-quality platform is essential for AI success.
*   **Discussion**: Do we have a "paved road" for development? Does our platform reduce cognitive load?

### 3. Fostering Trust (10 min)
*   **Review**: Strategies to address low trust (policies, feedback, exposure).
*   **Discussion**: Has our trust in AI changed over the last 6 weeks?

### 4. Measuring Success (10 min)
*   **Review**: Moving beyond simple metrics (like "lines of code") to outcomes (Flow, Satisfaction, Value).
*   **Discussion**: What metrics should we track going forward?

### 5. Team Commitments & Next Steps (10 min)
*   **Activity**:
    1. **Individual** (3 min): Write down ONE specific change you'll make
       - Format: "In the next 3 months, I will [action] to [outcome]"
       - Example: "I will reduce my PR size to <400 lines to improve code review quality"
    2. **Pair share** (4 min): Share with a partner for accountability
    3. **Team summary** (3 min): Facilitator captures top 3 team commitments
*   **Schedule 3-month check-in**: Add 30-min meeting to calendar for Week 18
*   **Deliverable**: Facilitator sends team commitments doc within 24 hours

## Key Questions
*   Do we have the foundational capabilities (like a good platform) to support AI?
*   Are we treating our internal platform as a product?

## Facilitator Notes

**If the commitment activity feels rushed**:
- Extend the meeting to 70 min to give proper time (10 min instead of 5)
- Make commitments in writing, not just verbal
- Use the format: "In the next 3 months, I will [action] to [outcome]"

**If discussion stalls on the 7 Capabilities**:
- Go through each capability and ask: "Rate us 1-5 on this. Why?"
- Focus on the weakest 2-3 capabilities, not all 7

**If no one did the reading**:
- Display the 7 Capabilities (already listed in Section 1) and define each briefly
- Shorten Section 2 (Platform) to 10 min

**If you run over time**:
- Priority order: Section 5 (Commitments) > Section 1 (Capabilities) > Section 3 (Trust) > Section 2
- Skip Section 4 (Metrics) if necessary—most teams know what to track by now

**Making commitments actionable**:
- Insist on specificity: "I'll be more careful" is not a commitment
- Good example: "I will reduce PR size to <400 lines"
- Bad example: "I'll use AI better"
- Schedule the 3-month check-in before people leave the meeting
