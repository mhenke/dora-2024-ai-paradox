## Agenda

### 1. Introduction &amp; Context (5 min)
*   **Set the context**: This book club explores AI's complex impact on software delivery.
*   **The Amplifier Effect**: The 2025 DORA findings show AI acts as an **amplifier**—it magnifies both our strengths (productivity, satisfaction) and our weaknesses (instability, batch size issues).
*   **The Central Question**: How do we use AI to amplify our strengths rather than our dysfunctions?

### 2. Review Pre-Work &amp; Current Friction Points (10 min)
*   **Discussion**:
    *   How are we using AI today?
    *   Where is our greatest current friction point in delivery?
    *   Do we feel our priorities are stable?

### 3. Define Core Metric for Tracking AI Impact (15 min)
*   **Decision**: As a team, decide on one quantitative metric to track over the 6 weeks.
*   **Goal**: Align with Success Criteria Level 3 - track this metric to see if it improves or degrades.
*   **Important**: If the metric gets worse, that's valuable data (not failure) - use it to diagnose which of the 7 Capabilities we need.

**Metric Selection Guidance**:

Good metrics are:
- **Measurable weekly**: Get signal fast (e.g., avg PR size, deployment frequency)
- **Influenced by AI use**: Should theoretically change as AI adoption changes
- **Team-owned**: You can actually change this metric through your practices

Examples:
- ✅ **Average PR size** - Easy to measure, AI clearly affects this
- ✅ **Change fail rate** - Weekly rollback count, stability indicator  
- ✅ **Time to merge** - From PR open to merged, tracks review speed
- ❌ **Lines of code** - Vanity metric, doesn't measure value
- ❌ **Deployment frequency** - Good metric but hard to move in 6 weeks
- ❌ **Customer satisfaction** - Too many external factors, slow signal

**Activity**: 
1. Brainstorm 3-5 potential metrics (5 min)
2. Vote on top 2 (2 min)
3. Agree on ONE to track, assign owner (3 min)

## Key Questions

*   Where is our greatest current friction point in delivery (the real bottleneck)?
*   What is the current team-wide consensus on trust in AI-generated code?
